隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 29 日

晴简天气由独立开发者以 SHIJIU STUDIO 品牌提供。我们尽量减少数据传输，并让你自主选择是否使用当前位置。

1. 位置与天气

只有你主动选择或刷新“当前位置”时，晴简才请求 iOS 的“使用 App 期间”定位权限，并取得一次位置。所选地点的查询坐标会发送给 Apple WeatherKit 获取天气；当前位置的地名会通过 Apple 的反向地理编码服务解析。固定城市可在不授予定位权限的情况下使用。晴简不持续记录位置轨迹，也不请求后台定位。

2. 城市搜索

全球城市搜索使用随 App 打包的 GeoNames 离线资料。搜索词只在设备上匹配，不发送给 GeoNames 或开发者的服务器。离线目录不包含所有街道和村镇。

3. 本机保存

晴简在设备上保存你选中和收藏的城市及其坐标、外观和语言偏好、最近的天气、地名与更新时间。天气缓存通过 App Group 与晴简小组件共享。晴简没有自己的账号、广告、分析或云同步服务器，不会将这些本机设置上传给开发者。

4. 服务不可用时

Apple WeatherKit 暂不可用时，晴简会显示上次天气缓存或明确的错误提示；当前版本不会请求备用天气服务。首次无可用缓存时可能显示标明为预览的数据。

5. 你的选择

你可以在 iOS“设置”中撤销晴简的定位权限，继续使用固定城市。删除 App 可移除其设备本地数据。第三方服务依其各自政策处理相关请求；可阅读 Apple 隐私政策。离线城市资料的来源及许可见 GeoNames。

6. 更新与联系

如将来增加会改变数据处理方式的功能，我们会在发布前更新本政策及 App Store 隐私资料。隐私问题请发送邮件至 shijiuhan19@gmail.com。

Qingjian Weather Privacy Policy

Updated October 29, 2026

1. Location and weather

Qingjian Weather is offered by an independent developer under the SHIJIU STUDIO brand. The app requests iOS When In Use location access only when you select or refresh Current Location. It obtains one location for that action. The selected coordinates are sent to Apple WeatherKit for forecasts, and Apple’s reverse geocoding service resolves the place name. You can use a saved city without location access. The app does not continuously record a location trail or request background location.

2. City search

City search matches a GeoNames data file bundled with the app. Search terms stay on the device and are not sent to GeoNames or a developer-operated server. The offline list does not contain every street or village.

3. Local storage

The app stores selected and favorite cities and their coordinates, appearance and language settings, recent weather, place names, and update times on your device. Weather cache is shared with its widgets through an App Group. The app has no developer-operated account, advertising, analytics, or cloud sync server, and does not upload these local settings to the developer.

4. Unavailable service

If Apple WeatherKit is unavailable, the app shows cached weather or a clear error. The current version does not use a backup weather provider. With no cache, it may show data clearly labeled as a preview.

5. Your choices

You can revoke location access in iOS Settings and continue using saved cities. Deleting the app removes its on-device app data. External services handle requests under their own policies: see Apple Privacy Policy. The bundled city data and its license are described by GeoNames.

6. Updates and contact

We will update this policy and the App Store privacy answers before shipping features that change data handling. For privacy questions, email shijiuhan19@gmail.com.
