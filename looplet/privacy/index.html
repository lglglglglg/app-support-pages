Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Looplet is built by the independent developer brand SHIJIU STUDIO. We minimize data access by design.

1. Data we do not collect

Looplet does not require an account and currently includes no advertising, tracking, analytics, or cloud sync. We do not collect, upload, sell, or share personal information.

2. Screen-recording permission

Looplet needs macOS Screen & System Audio Recording permission to read the screen area you deliberately select. The permission is used only for a recording you initiate.

3. On-device processing

Selected frames, still-frame detection, and GIF encoding are processed on your Mac. Screen content is not uploaded to us or to third-party servers.

4. Files and clipboard

  • Saving a GIF writes only to the location selected in the macOS save panel.
  • Copying or dragging a GIF may create a file in the Mac's temporary directory.
  • You can delete exported and temporary files using standard macOS controls.

5. Apple services and external links

Looplet does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, account, or cloud-storage SDKs. Apple processes Looplet Pro purchases, restores, and payment credentials through the App Store and StoreKit. External links open only after you choose them.

6. Local usage records

The free edition stores daily and total successful-capture counters on this Mac to enforce the daily allowance and introductory watermark-free captures. These records are not uploaded. Pro status is confirmed from App Store transaction data.

7. Your controls

You can disable Looplet in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording. Recording will stop working, but previously saved GIFs are unaffected.

8. Policy updates

If Looplet later adds networking, accounts, diagnostics, or another feature that changes data handling, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy answers before the change ships.

9. Contact

Email shijiuhan19@gmail.com with privacy questions. Do not attach passwords, credentials, or unrelated private screen content.

Looplet 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 13 日

1. 我们不收集的数据

Looplet 不要求注册账号，不包含广告、用户追踪、行为分析或云端同步，也不会收集、上传、出售或共享个人信息。

2. 屏幕录制权限

Looplet 需要 macOS 的“屏幕与系统音频录制”权限，才能读取你主动框选的屏幕区域。该权限只用于你主动发起的录制。

3. 本机处理

画面帧、静止检测和 GIF 编码均在设备本地完成，不会上传给开发者或第三方服务器。

4. 文件与剪贴板

保存 GIF 时只写入系统保存面板中指定的位置；复制或拖出 GIF 时可能在本机临时目录创建文件，你可通过 macOS 的常规方式删除。

5. Apple 服务与外部链接

Looplet 不集成第三方分析、广告、账号或云存储 SDK。Looplet Pro 的购买、恢复和付款凭据由 Apple 的 App Store 与 StoreKit 处理。外部链接只在你主动点击后打开。

6. 本机使用记录

免费版会在本机保存每日及累计成功生成次数，用于每日额度和初始免水印体验；这些记录不会上传。Pro 状态由 App Store 交易数据确认。

7. 你的控制权

你可以在“系统设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 屏幕与系统音频录制”中关闭权限。关闭后无法继续录制，但不影响已经保存的 GIF。

8. 政策更新

如后续加入联网、账号、诊断或其他改变数据处理方式的功能，我们会在发布前更新本政策和 App Store 隐私问卷。

9. 联系方式

隐私问题请发送邮件至 shijiuhan19@gmail.com。请勿附带密码、凭据或无关的私人屏幕内容。
