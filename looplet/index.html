Looplet Support

Help with screen recording, GIF export, Looplet Pro purchases, and general feedback.

Email support

shijiuhan19@gmail.com · English and Chinese

Before contacting support

  • Include your macOS version and Looplet version/build number.
  • Describe the steps that led to the problem and what you expected.
  • For screen-recording issues, confirm Looplet is enabled in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording.
  • Never send passwords, purchase credentials, API keys, or unrelated private screen content.

Purchases

Looplet Pro is sold only as an in-app purchase through the Mac App Store. Prices, payment, refunds, and purchase restoration are handled by Apple. We do not sell external license keys and will never ask you to bypass the App Store.
