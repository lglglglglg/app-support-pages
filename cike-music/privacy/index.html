隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 2 日

此刻音乐由独立开发者以 SHIJIU STUDIO 品牌提供。应用以本地优先方式管理你选择的音乐文件，并可按你的配置连接个人音乐服务器。

1. 本地音乐与资料库

此刻音乐不要求注册开发者账号，不接入广告、用户追踪或第三方行为分析。只有在你主动选择文件或文件夹后，应用才会读取其中的音频和相关歌词、封面，用于建立本机索引和播放。应用默认不会修改原始音乐文件，也不会把本地音乐、文件路径、播放历史、收藏或歌单发送给开发者。

2. 设备内保存与备份

曲库索引、播放记录、收藏、歌单、播放断点、外观设置和图片皮肤保存在你的设备上。你主动导出的备份可能包含本地文件绝对路径，但不包含原始音乐文件。请只把备份保存到你信任的位置。删除应用及其数据会移除这些本地资料；macOS 备份的处理取决于你的系统设置。

3. 个人远程曲库

当你添加 Navidrome 或 OpenSubsonic 来源时，服务器地址、用户名和曲库索引保存在设备内，密码保存在 Apple 系统钥匙串。播放、同步和搜索请求由应用直接发送到你指定的服务器；开发者不代管服务器账号，也不接收你的音乐。你选择的服务器运营者及网络服务商可能依其政策处理请求、IP 地址和日志。

4. 外部链接与主动联系

只有你主动打开帮助页、公开隐私政策、支持邮箱或 QQ 群时，系统才会打开相应外部服务。GitHub Pages、邮件服务或 QQ 可能依各自政策处理访问或联系信息。反馈时请勿发送密码、访问令牌、私人音乐文件或无关私密资料。

5. 政策更新与联系

若未来加入会改变数据处理方式的功能，我们会在发布前更新本政策。隐私问题请联系 shijiuhan19@gmail.com。

Cike Music Privacy Policy

Updated November 2, 2026

1. Local music and library

Cike Music is offered by an independent developer under the SHIJIU STUDIO brand. It requires no developer account and includes no advertising, user tracking, or third-party behavioral analytics. It reads audio, lyrics, and artwork only from files or folders you choose. It does not send local music, paths, listening history, favorites, or playlists to the developer.

2. On-device storage and backups

Library indexes, listening history, favorites, playlists, playback checkpoints, appearance settings, and custom skins are stored on your device. A backup you explicitly export may contain absolute local file paths but does not contain the original music. Store backups only in locations you trust.

3. Personal remote libraries

For Navidrome or OpenSubsonic sources, the server address, username, and library index are stored on device, while the password is stored in Apple Keychain. The app connects directly to the server you specify. The developer does not proxy your requests or receive your music. Your server operator and network provider may process requests, IP addresses, and logs under their own policies.

4. External links and contact

External support, privacy, email, or QQ services open only when you choose them. GitHub Pages, your email provider, or QQ may process visit or contact information under their policies. Do not send passwords, access tokens, private music files, or unrelated sensitive information in feedback.

5. Updates and contact

We will update this policy before shipping features that change data handling. For privacy questions, email shijiuhan19@gmail.com.
