隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 2 日

知节历由独立开发者以 SHIJIU STUDIO 品牌提供。当前版本以设备内日期计算和随应用发布的节假日资料工作。

1. 应用内的数据

知节历不要求注册账号，不读取通讯录、相册、定位或系统日历，不接入广告、用户追踪或第三方行为分析。当前版本不会向开发者发送你的日期查看记录或使用偏好。

2. 设备内保存

外观、每周起始日、农历与黄历显示开关、节假日高亮开关保存在设备本地。农历、节气和黄历由本地算法计算；已收录的法定节假日及调休资料随 App 发布。“今天”小组件使用本地日期计算和随组件发布的节假日资料，不需要账号或开发者服务器。删除 App 可移除其本地偏好；设备备份的处理取决于你的 iOS 备份设置。

3. 外部链接与主动联系

只有你主动点开支持页、公开隐私政策、QQ 群或官方通知链接时，iOS 才会打开对应的外部网站；网站服务商可能依其政策处理访问数据。知节历不会因显示日历而自动访问这些网站。若你主动发送邮件或加入 QQ 群，邮件或 QQ 服务会处理你选择提供的联系信息和内容。反馈时请勿发送无关的私密资料。

4. 更新与联系

若将来加入联网更新或其他改变数据处理方式的功能，我们会在发布前更新本政策和相应的 App Store 隐私资料。隐私问题请联系 shijiuhan19@gmail.com。

ZhiJieLi Privacy Policy

Updated November 2, 2026

1. Data in the app

ZhiJieLi is offered by an independent developer under the SHIJIU STUDIO brand. It requires no account and does not access contacts, photos, location, or your system calendar. It has no ads, user tracking, or third-party behavioral analytics. The current app does not send viewed dates or preferences to the developer.

2. On-device storage

Appearance, first day of the week, lunar and almanac display options, and holiday highlighting preferences are saved on your device. Lunar dates, solar terms, and traditional almanac information are calculated locally. Verified holiday schedules are bundled with the app. The Today widget uses local date calculations and bundled holiday data. Deleting the app removes its local preferences; iOS backup behavior depends on your device settings.

3. External links and contact

iOS opens a third-party website only when you choose a support, privacy, QQ group, or official notice link. That website may process visit data under its own policy. Merely viewing the calendar does not visit these sites. If you choose to email us or join the QQ group, the relevant service processes the information you provide. Please avoid sending unrelated private information.

4. Updates and contact

We will update this policy and the App Store privacy information before shipping features that change data handling. For privacy questions, email shijiuhan19@gmail.com.
