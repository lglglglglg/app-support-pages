隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 16 日 · English

小象发现家是面向家庭的儿童绘本 App。本政策说明当前版本在 App 内怎样保存记录，以及你主动打开外部页面或联系支持时会发生什么。

1. App 内的本机数据

阅读页数、读完记录、阅读与声音偏好、所选称号保存在这台设备的 App 数据中；印章根据本机完成记录显示。它们用于继续阅读和显示成长记录，不会自动上传到我们运营的服务器。当前 App 不要求注册账号，也不提供开发者运营的云同步。

当前 App 不接入广告、第三方行为分析、用户追踪或 App 内购买；不请求麦克风、相机或位置权限。我们不会通过 App 收集孩子的姓名、照片、位置、联系方式或阅读记录。

2. 绘本、音频与系统朗读

绘本图文及部分预录音频随 App 提供。部分短绘本和提示使用 iOS 系统朗读。App 不把朗读文本或声音录音发送给我们；系统语音的可用性和设备下载设置由 iOS 管理。App 不录制孩子的语音。

3. 主动打开的网站和邮件

家长区域提供支持、隐私、使用条款和语音来源链接；语音来源包含四个 Hugging Face 页面。打开这些链接后，设备会连接到相应网站，网站运营方可能按其政策处理 IP 地址、浏览器信息等访问数据。访问本支持站点时，GitHub Pages 也可能处理网页访问数据。这些访问与 App 本机阅读记录分开，App 不向这些网站发送阅读进度。

如果家长主动发送支持邮件，邮件服务会处理发件邮箱地址及家长选择提供的内容，我们会用这些信息回复并处理问题。邮件仅在处理请求和必要的后续跟进期间保留；家长可联系下方邮箱请求删除我们持有的邮件，法律要求保留的情况除外。请不要在邮件里发送孩子的姓名、照片、位置、学校或其他不必要的个人信息。

4. 保存、删除与备份

本机阅读记录通常保留到你删除 App。删除 App 通常会移除设备上的 App 数据；iOS 的“卸载 App”会保留数据，和删除不同。已有 iOS 设备备份可能包含 App 数据，删除 App 不会自动删除旧备份；请在设备和备份设置中自行管理。我们没有这些记录的服务器副本，不能远程查看、恢复或代删本机进度。

5. 儿童与家长

请由家长或监护人选择适龄内容、管理外部链接，并由成人联系支持。App 不要求孩子提供个人资料，不提供聊天、用户发布内容或向开发者上传作品的功能。若未来加入会改变数据处理方式的功能，我们会在发布前更新本政策和 App Store 隐私申报。

6. 联系方式与更新

如对本政策、数据处理或内容来源有疑问，请由成人联系 shijiuhan19@gmail.com。政策变更会在本页标明更新日期；重大数据处理变化会随 App 更新说明。

Xiaoxiang Faxianjia Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Xiaoxiang Faxianjia is a picture-book app for families. This policy explains data stored within the current app and what happens when an adult chooses to open an external site or contact support.

1. Data stored on your device

Reading position, completed books, reading and voice preferences, and the selected title are stored in this device's app data. Badges are displayed from local completion records. These support resume reading and the growth screen. The app does not automatically upload them to a server we operate. No account or developer-run cloud sync is offered.

The current app has no advertising, third-party behavioral analytics, tracking, or in-app purchases. It does not request microphone, camera, or location permission. We do not collect a child's name, photos, location, contact details, or reading history through the app.

2. Books, audio, and system speech

Book text and illustrations, and some prerecorded audio, are bundled with the app. Some short books and prompts use iOS system speech. The app does not send narration text or voice recordings to us. iOS manages system voice availability and device download settings. The app does not record children speaking.

3. Websites and email you choose to open

The adult area contains links to support, privacy, terms, and four Hugging Face pages about voice sources. Opening a link connects your device to that website. Its operator may process IP address, browser details, and other visit information under its own policy. GitHub Pages may likewise process visits to this support website. These visits are separate from local reading history; the app does not send reading progress to those sites.

If a parent or guardian emails support, the email provider processes the sender address and content they choose to provide. We use that information to reply and address the request. We keep support emails only while handling the request and necessary follow-up. An adult may ask us to delete the emails we hold, unless retention is required by law. Please do not include a child's name, photo, location, school, or other unnecessary personal information.

4. Retention, deletion, and backups

Local reading data normally remains until you delete the app. Deleting the app normally removes its data from that device. Offloading the app retains data and is different from deletion. Existing iOS device backups may contain app data and are not automatically erased when you delete the app; manage them in device and backup settings. We have no server copy of these records and cannot remotely view, restore, or erase local progress.

5. Children and parents

Parents or guardians should choose suitable content, manage external links, and contact support themselves. The app does not ask children for personal profiles or provide chat, user-posted content, or uploads to us. Before introducing features that change data handling, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure.

6. Contact and policy updates

For privacy, data, or content-source questions, an adult may contact shijiuhan19@gmail.com. Changes will be dated on this page; material changes to data handling will accompany an app update notice.
