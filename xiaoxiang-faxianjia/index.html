常见问题

没有网络，还能看绘本吗？

可以。当前版本的绘本、插画和预录音频随 App 提供，核心阅读不依赖开发者服务器。打开语音来源网页、支持页面或发送邮件需要网络。设备系统朗读的可用声音与表现取决于 iOS 设置。

为什么有些故事的声音不同？

部分较长故事使用随 App 提供、预先制作的 AI 合成旁白；低龄短绘本和部分提示使用 iOS 系统朗读；英语词句使用预录发音。请检查设备音量和静音设置；若某页始终没有声音，请在邮件中说明绘本名称、页码与设备系统版本。

阅读进度与印章在哪里？

进度、读完记录、偏好和所选称号保存在这台设备的 App 数据中；印章根据本机完成记录显示。App 不提供账号或开发者运营的云同步，换设备后不保证自动转移；iOS 系统备份是否能恢复这些数据取决于你的备份设置。

怎样删除本机记录？

删除 App 通常会移除这台设备上的 App 数据。“卸载 App”保留数据，与“删除 App”不同。已有 iOS 设备备份可能仍保留旧数据，请在设备备份设置中自行管理。开发者没有云端阅读记录可代为删除。

适合什么年龄？

每本绘本的建议年龄与读法各有不同，低龄内容更适合亲子共读。家长可先看家长说明，再依孩子的兴趣、理解能力和情绪反应选择内容；年龄建议不是测评结论。

Frequently asked questions

Can we read without an internet connection?

Yes. Current books, illustrations, and prerecorded audio are bundled with the app. Opening an external source or support page, or sending email, needs a connection. Availability and sound quality of iOS system speech depend on your device settings.

Why do voices differ between books?

Some longer stories use bundled, prerecorded AI-synthesized narration. Shorter books and some prompts use iOS system speech. English words and phrases use prerecorded pronunciation. If audio fails on a particular page, check device volume and include the book title, page number, device, and iOS version in your email.

Where is reading progress stored?

Reading progress, completion, preferences, and the selected title stay in this device's app data. Badges are displayed from local completion records. There is no account or developer-run cloud sync. Transfer through an iOS device backup depends on your backup settings and is not guaranteed.

How do I remove local records?

Deleting the app normally removes its data from this device. Offloading an app retains data and is different from deleting it. Existing iOS device backups may still contain earlier data and are managed in your device backup settings. We have no cloud reading history to delete for you.

What age is it for?

Books have different suggested ages and reading modes. Younger children may benefit from reading with a parent or guardian. Choose content according to the child's interests, understanding, and comfort; age guidance is not an assessment.
